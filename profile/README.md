# Centro Innova

**Open experimentation for integration, interoperability, and digital innovation.**

The **Centro Innova**, located at the **University of Vale do Rio dos Sinos (UNISINOS), Brazil**, is an open innovation center specialized in the **integration and interoperability testing of products, platforms, and digital services**.

Our mission is to provide a collaborative experimentation environment where academia, industry, government, and technology communities can develop, integrate, test, validate, and demonstrate innovative solutions.

## Our Focus

Centro Innova brings together research, experimentation, and innovation in areas such as:

- **5G and 6G Networks**
- **Open RAN**
- **Network Slicing and Network-as-a-Service**
- **Cloud and Edge Computing**
- **Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems**
- **Network Automation and Orchestration**
- **Open APIs and Programmable Networks**
- **Broadband and Network Interoperability**
- **Cybersecurity**
- **Internet of Things (IoT)**
- **Robotics and Autonomous Systems**
- **Extended Reality (XR) and Immersive Applications**
- **Distributed and Cloud-Native Systems**

## Integration and Interoperability

A core objective of Centro Innova is to create an open environment where technologies from different vendors, platforms, and research projects can be integrated and evaluated together.

Our activities include:

- integration testing;
- interoperability testing;
- experimental validation;
- performance and scalability evaluation;
- proof-of-concept development;
- open testbeds;
- technology demonstrations;
- Living Labs;
- collaborative experimentation with industry and academia.

## Open Experimentation Ecosystem

Centro Innova promotes an open experimentation ecosystem that connects researchers, students, companies, startups, technology providers, standardization organizations, open-source communities, and public institutions.

By combining **communication networks, cloud-edge computing, artificial intelligence, robotics, cybersecurity, and immersive technologies**, the center supports the development and validation of next-generation digital infrastructures and intelligent systems.

## Open Source and Reproducible Research

One of Centro Innova's goals is to strengthen open and reproducible research.

Through this GitHub organization, we aim to support:

- sharing of experimental configurations;
- reproducibility of scientific experiments;
- reuse of software components;
- collaborative software development;
- sharing of datasets and technical documentation;
- integration between academia and industry;
- dissemination of research and innovation results.
